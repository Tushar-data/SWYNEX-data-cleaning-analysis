💡 Key Insights

* Month-to-month customers had a higher churn rate (35%) compared with one-year (21.3%) and two-year (14.7%) contracts.
* Churned customers had an average tenure of 30.51 months, compared with 38.55 months for retained customers.
* Churned customers had slightly higher average monthly charges (₹79.53) than retained customers (₹76.40).
* Overall customer churn in the dataset was 27.6%.

🎯 Final Conclusion

The analysis highlights how contract type, customer tenure and monthly charges can be examined to understand churn patterns and identify customer segments that may require greater retention attention.
